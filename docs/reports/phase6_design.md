## The predictions

Set by the orchestrator in prompt 85 on 2026-11-15, and committed alone before the probe runs
(commit fe2d757).

- (a) Scaling a tolerance by 1% moves no detection latency by more than one time step on either
  grid.
- (b) The 1e-15 inflow perturbation leaves every decision output unchanged and moves no field by
  more than 1e-10 relative.
- (c) The tolerance changes move fields by about the tolerances' size: no field moves by more
  than 1e-6 relative.

## How they are scored (the builder, before the runs)

The reference is main's code and configuration unchanged on each grid. A variant is one change
to it: `solver.pressure_rtol` or `transport.diffusion_tol` times 0.99 or 1.01 (the four
tolerance variants), every velocity inlet's velocity times 1 + 1e-15 (the inflow variant), and
the reduction this pass chooses. Each variant is set against the reference on the same grid
and scenario.

- **A detection latency** is every one the system reports or a search reads: the file's four
  sensors' latencies from each event's onset per channel (the monitor), every non-SOLID cell's
  first time at or above per event and channel (the arrival maps, which are the latency of a
  sensor at that cell), and each searched layout's latency per unit. Two latencies are compared
  in steps: the difference in seconds over the step of the interval the later of the two falls
  in. A latency reached in one run and never in the other has moved by more than one step.
  (a) holds when no latency of any of the four tolerance variants moves by more than one step.
- **A decision output** for (b) is: every detection latency as above, compared by its step;
  every cell's clear time and the room's clear time per all-clear unit
  (`placement.clear_units`, frame-resolved), compared by its frame; the searched layouts' cells
  under both rules and every candidate pool, and the order of the four layouts (the file's,
  the surface search, the product layout, the free optimum) under each rule; and each class's
  verdict against REQ-T05 (the accumulated leftover and the residual within 1e-4 of the
  supply). "Unchanged" is equal by those comparisons; times themselves can move in their last
  bits, since the step follows the flow.
- **A field's relative difference** is the largest absolute difference over the cells over the
  largest magnitude of the reference's field: per flow state for u, v, p, k, eps and nu_t, and
  per class over every stored frame (the largest difference over the frames over the largest
  value over the frames). (b) and (c) are scored on the largest of these over every field,
  state, class, scenario and grid of the variants they name.

## 1. ADR-017

ADR-017 records Alex's six decisions as given, each with the profile's evidence
(`docs/reports/profile84.md`): Linux under WSL2 as the target; the whole step in C++ with the
fields resident, since the plan's two kernels bound a run's speedup at 2.29 to 2.76 (profile
T12); the order CPU core, then CUDA on the 2D problem, then 3D through a Phase 6 ECR; code
written per direction with only 2D built; double precision on the CPU, single precision in CUDA
only where measured to pay; parity at two levels. It then proposes, each with alternatives and
a recommendation: the toolchain (A), the binding (B), the core's modules and their interfaces
mapped to today's `src/` (C), the tests (D), what stays in Python and what crosses (E), what the
core must carry exactly and the scope of the port (F), threads and determinism (G), the
end-to-end criterion from this probe (H), REQ-N03 and REQ-S06 restated (I), the plan (J), and
CLAUDE.md's compiled-code rules (K).

Two facts of this machine shape section A. WSL2 holds only Docker Desktop's distribution, so
the toolchain step starts by installing one; and the NVIDIA driver (621.14) supports CUDA 13.5
on the RTX 4070 Laptop GPU. The CI already runs the Python suite on `ubuntu-latest`, so the CPU
core can be built and tested there with no new runner.

## 2. The noise band

### What was run

Each run is `time_integration.run` from rest, in its own process on one BLAS thread, at most
three at once (`docs/reports/probe85/run85.py`): the dust release, the door leak and the filter
breach on 80x30, and the dust release and the breach on 160x60 (the deviation above), each as
the reference, a repeat of it, and seven variants (`docs/reports/probe85/probe85.py`):

- the four tolerance variants and the inflow variant the prompt names;
- the reduction this pass chose, `leftover_sequential`: the implicit transport solve's
  leftover, the one sum ADR-016's stop reads, taken left to right over the cells in row-major
  order as a plain C++ loop takes it, in place of NumPy's pairwise sum. ADR-016's consequences
  name this sum as the one a port must reproduce exactly. The patch also counts the sums the
  order changed and the stop verdicts it would have flipped (P7);
- `outer_one_later`, added by this pass beyond the prompt's list: each flow solve runs one more
  outer iteration past the first at which its stopping rule says stop. No variant the prompt
  names moved the outer stop (P1: every outer count is the reference's), and a port can, since
  the rule's conditions (c) and (d) read sums over the cells. It is reported in the band (P8)
  and kept out of the predictions' scoring, which the variants the prompt named decide.

The 80x30 configurations are prompt 79's placement files' in content, so the references can be
checked against prompt 81's runs of them (P1b).

**Proposed as the end-to-end criterion** (ADR-017 section H, for Alex's acceptance): every
decision equal, a decision admissible otherwise only where the reference's own deciding
reading lies within the field limit of its threshold; every field within ten times the largest
spread P8 measured, rounded up to a decade (the flow fields 1e-8, the class fields, their
pointwise reading at the thresholds' decades and the sensor channels 1e-6, the accumulated
leftover 1e-8 of the supply); every flow stop within one outer iteration of the reference's.
The factor of ten is a choice, and ADR-017 H gives the alternatives.

**What a port result outside it would mean.** A decision that differs where the reference's
reading is not marginal is a defect: the port computes something the reference does not, and
the level 1 tests on the inputs of the step where the fields first leave the band find the
block. Fields outside a limit with every decision equal are either a defect that has not yet
reached a decision or more stops differing than the margin covers; the iteration counts
separate the two, since counts within one of the reference's with fields outside the limit put
the fault in the arithmetic, and counts further apart put it in a stop that is not computed as
ADR-017 F states. The band is not widened to fit a port; a change shown to be legitimate and
outside it is added to the reference as a variant and the band remeasured.

**What the band does not cover.** It is measured on 80x30 and 160x60, the prompt's grids;
the end-to-end step reruns the two stop variants on the 320x120 dust release before scoring
there (`docs/PROJECT_PLAN.md`, Phase 6 step 6). And single precision rounds at about 6e-8,
the size of the class-field limits before any accumulation, so under decision 6's "the same
benchmark" the field limits will likely exclude a single-precision stage that the decisions
accept (question 8 below).

## Findings

1. **No legitimate change the probe made moved a decision**, on either grid or any scenario:
   no reached arrival time left its step, no clear time its frame, no searched layout its cells,
   the order of the layouts held, and every budget stayed inside REQ-T05 (P3 to P6).
2. **The fields move by two amounts**: about 1e-12 under rounding carried through a run, and up
   to a few times 1e-8 (class fields) and about 1e-9 (flow fields) under a stop one iteration or
   sweep apart (P2, P8). The second sets the band.
3. **The reference reproduces itself**: a repeat in another process is bitwise on every run, and
   the 80x30 references, solved from rest, reproduce prompt 81's runs, which copied their flow
   states, bit for bit (P1, P1b).
4. **The leftover's order matters to its last bits and to no stop**: most leftovers formed differ
   between the two orders, by up to about 2e-11 of their size, and none of the stop tests flipped
   (P7). ADR-016's caution, that a port must reproduce this stop exactly, is not contradicted: no
   flip here does not mean none can occur, and the band admits the field change a flip would make.
5. **The three predictions hold** (P9). (a) holds on the arrival maps and the searched layouts;
   the file's four sensors are never reached, so they add nothing to it.

## Deviations and limits

- The door leak was not run on 160x60 (the deviation above); the breach was added on both grids.
- `outer_one_later` is a variant beyond the prompt's list, added because no named variant moved
  the outer stop. It is in the band and outside the predictions' scoring.
- The inflow variant scales every velocity inlet, the scenario's own included (the door's
  corridor inflow, the breach's three panels), so that each flow state's inflow is perturbed;
  the outlets are fixed-flow and take the balance.
- The band is measured on 80x30 and 160x60 only; 320x120 is left to the end-to-end step.
- Which pressure corrections' CG counts the pressure tolerance changed was not recorded (two
  of its runs are bitwise, P1), and neither was which class steps' Jacobi counts the diffusion
  tolerance changed.
- The full test suite was not run: nothing under `src/` or `tests/` changed. The probe's own
  tests (`docs/reports/probe85/test_probe85.py`) pass.
