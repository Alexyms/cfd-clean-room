## The case and the runs

Sohankar, Norberg and Davidson (1998): a unit square cylinder at 5% blockage,
U = 1, the inlet 10 d upstream, the Neumann outlet 26 d downstream, here with
freestream lateral boundaries (ADR-014 F). `configs/validation_cylinder.yaml` on
the clustered grid of decision 11 (0.025 d at the body's faces, at most 0.3 d in
the far field, 280 by 150 cells); the uniform 0.1 d grid of the probe (370 by
200) is the coarse rung. BDF2 at dt 0.05 with an implicit Euler first step, each
step's outer iterations to 1e-4 U (ADR-014 decisions 3 to 5). Every row starts
from the probe's perturbed start-up (200 steady outer iterations from rest with
the lower boundary at 0.9 U, `validation.cylinder.perturbed_startup`; on the
uniform grid it is the probe's kept field to the byte), and ran through
`scripts/benchmark.py`'s cylinder family at commit 6fe887b with fifteen
processes sharing the machine (the four rows and the eleven hash solves).

The measures were stated in `validation/cylinder.py` before the runs: Re 40
steady when the lift's last five amplitudes each fall, or, where the lift no
longer completes six periods in the run's second half, when its largest
magnitude over the last tenth is below 1e-6; Re 60 shedding when each of the
last five amplitudes is at least 0.99 of the one before; Re 100 when the
Strouhal number of the last ten periods lies in 0.139 to 0.153 (Alex,
2026-10-17).

## The rows (tables V1 to V4, V6)

- **Re 100 on the clustered grid passes.** V2: the Strouhal number of the last
  ten periods is inside the band, and the spectrum's peak agrees within its
  resolution; the drag coefficient is inside the paper's 1.45 to 1.49. V4: the
  period settles over the run's middle third and is constant to five figures
  over the last ten periods; V6: the lift's amplitude and the drag are constant
  over the last hundred units.
- **Re 60 passes.** V3: the last six amplitudes are equal to four figures, the
  lift saturated; V6: it grew over the first hundred units from the start-up.
- **Re 40 fails by the measure stated.** V3: the lift completes too few periods
  in the run's second half, so the quiet reading applies, and its largest
  magnitude over the last tenth is about a hundred times the 1e-6 set before the
  run. V6 shows why: the lift falls by a factor of about forty from the first
  window to the third while the drag settles to five figures, and over the last fifty units
  every step takes one outer iteration, its first already within the per-step criterion of
  1e-4 U, so the march no longer iterates and a lift below about 1e-4 is not
  resolved. The 1e-6 threshold took rounding as the floor; the floor of a march
  at that criterion is the criterion. The flow is steady by its drag and by the
  outer counts, not by the lift measure as stated, and the measure is not
  changed after the run: the choice is the orchestrator's (a quiet level tied to
  the per-step criterion, or the decay read while the steps still iterate).
- **The uniform rung** reads a Strouhal number inside the band too, about 4.4%
  below the clustered grid's, and a larger drag coefficient (V2).

## The budget

The rule was every VAL-022 run of the pass together under 8 hours, estimated
from the first few hundred steps of each grid. The estimate (V5, four processes
sharing the machine, 300 steps from the start-up) put the four runs at about 5.2
hours summed and about 2 hours of wall clock run side by side, and the runs went
ahead on it. They took about 3.2 hours of wall clock, but summed over the four
processes about 9.1 hours (V1's wall column): once shedding develops a step
takes 8 to 9 outer iterations against the 5 the first 300 steps showed (V6),
and fifteen processes shared the machine against the estimate's four. Read as
summed process time the budget was exceeded; read as wall time it held.
